## What CFF Is

College Fast Forward (CFF) is a career platform that connects college students with parents and alumni who are willing to help them get hired. It's not a job board — it's a warm-introduction network with AI tools layered on top.

**Core premise:** One warm intro beats 100 cold applications. CFF provides the warm intros, plus the AI tools to make them count.

**Two audiences:**
- **Students/Gators:** Looking for connections, career advice, resume help, mock interviews, referrals
- **Parents & Alumni (helpers):** Professionals who want to pay it forward to students at their school

---

## FastIQ

FastIQ is CFF's AI-powered career engine. It's a separate premium product layered on top of the free CFF network.

**What FastIQ does:**
- Discovers alumni at target companies (not just publicly posted)
- Drafts personalized outreach messages that get replies
- Tailors resumes to specific job descriptions
- Runs full STAR method mock interview practice
- Reviews LinkedIn profiles
- Provides company hiring signals and salary intel
- Career archetype assessment
- Weekly scout — new alumni matches delivered automatically
- Action plan and daily prioritization

**Positioning:** "While you sleep, FastIQ is working." FastIQ is for students who want to stop cold-applying and start getting responses.

## Seasonal Context (April 2026)

- **Graduation** is 4–6 weeks away for most students
- **Summer internships** are starting in 6–10 weeks
- **Spring recruiting cycle** is closing — final hiring decisions happening now
- **Marketing angle:** Students either want to put in the work now or they don't. This is the last real window before summer. Urgency is authentic, not manufactured.

**Seasonal messaging that works:**
- "Graduation is 6 weeks away. Are you ready?"
- "Summer internships start in June. How's the search going?"
- "This is the window. After graduation it gets harder."

**Seasonal messaging to avoid:**
- Generic "busy season" copy that doesn't connect to actual timeline
- Catastrophizing — the tone is honest urgency, not fear

## Voice and Tone

**Jill's voice (from Jill, founder-to-founder):**
- Direct and personal
- No corporate speak
- Honest about what the platform can and can't do
- Warm but not saccharine
- Uses short sentences, plain language
- Signs off "Warmly, Jill" or "— Jill"

**CFF brand voice:**
- Confident but not arrogant
- Evidence-based ("47 students sent 200+ intros")
- Treats students as adults making real decisions
- Never infantilizing ("good luck on your job search!")
- Parent voice: respectful, peer-to-peer, "you know what it takes"

## What CFF Is NOT

- Not a job board — no listings
- Not a staffing agency — no guarantees of placement
- Not a coaching service — CFF provides tools and connections, not hand-holding
- Not free for FastIQ — the AI tools require a subscription (the network is free forever)
